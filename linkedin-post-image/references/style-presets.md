# Style presets (derived from the reference LinkedIn designs)

All sizes are for a 1080x1350 canvas. Colors are approximations; adjust to the user's brand if given.

## 1. Bold Question (minimal, white + electric blue)
Pattern: "Does Your **WORD** Feel **WORD**?" with one hero image below.
- bg `#F7F8FA`, text `#111`, accent `#0A6CFF`
- Fonts: small words "Poppins" 500 at 44px (black); hero word "Archivo" 900, font-stretch 125% (or "Unbounded" 800), 150px, accent blue; second keyword 56px, accent blue, uppercase
- Layout: centered text block in top 40%; bottom 40% = single hero visual (CSS gradient mountain, umbrella row, water ripple or user image) fading into the white background
- Footer: tiny "role + name" credit, centered, 22px
- Best for: hook posts, brand/positioning questions

## 2. Quote Portrait (quote card + photo)
- bg light gray `#EDEDED` or dark `#14181F`, card `#3B4A40` (green) or `#E3E3E3`, accent `#4DB96B` or `#0A8F4D`
- Fonts: quote "Montserrat" 600 italic or "Manrope" 500, 64px; name "Montserrat" 800, 56px, accent color; role 28px
- Layout: large accent quote marks overlapping a rounded rectangle (radius 40px) holding the quote; portrait cut-out on one side (user-supplied only); name + role below
- No photo supplied: use a solid accent shape or initials circle instead

## 3. Neon Cyber Cheat Sheet (dark + lime)
- bg `#050505` with radial lime glow `rgba(214,255,0,.35)` top-right, text `#FFFFFF`, accent `#D6FF00`
- Fonts: headings "Anton" or "Bebas Neue" 120px uppercase; accent word on a lime-filled block; italic serif ("Playfair Display" 700 italic) for emphasis words; items "Inter" 600 32px
- Layout: heading top, then a vertical stack of 4-5 pill rows (border 1px `rgba(255,255,255,.35)`, radius 10px, padding 16px 28px, dark translucent fill)
- Slide pattern: cover, intro line, one slide per audience/category, CTA

## 4. Dot-Grid Stat Card (editorial, white + blue)
- bg `#F6F6F6` with dot grid (`radial-gradient(#cfcfcf 1.5px, transparent 1.5px)` size 28px), text `#0B0B0B`, accent `#3A68FF`
- Fonts: "Poppins" 700 headline 84px (tight tracking -2%), body 32px 500
- Layout: top-left tag (brand), top-right arrow pill; stat cards in accent blue (radius 28px, white text, big number 72px); bottom-left pill tag, bottom-right counter "03/04" pill (border 2px black, radius 999px)
- Best for: results, case studies, numbers

## 5. Dark Glass Testimonial
- bg `#18191C`, card `#1C1D21` radius 48px border 1px `rgba(255,255,255,.08)`, text `#F2F2F2`, muted `#8A8D94`
- Fonts: "Poppins" 300 quote 64px, name 500 32px, role 400 30px muted
- Layout: card centered; avatar in top-right as a quarter-circle crop on a deep green `#1F4D1F` tile; two angular quote marks (gray gradient) above the text
- Use only testimonials supplied by the user

## 6. Insight Statement (navy + highlight)
- bg `#14102A`, text `#FFFFFF`, highlight `#8478E0`, dot `#FF2E93`
- Fonts: "Montserrat" 800 at 140px, letter-spacing -4%; subtext "Montserrat" 500 36px
- Layout: huge left-aligned statement; one key phrase in italic on a slightly rotated (-1deg) highlight box; pink period at the end; top-left logo mark, top-right small caps label, bottom-right thin arrow "→" (swipe cue)

## 7. Blue Frame Motivation
- bg `#0B2FB5` with subtle noise and large faint shapes, panel `#FFFFFF`, text accent `#0B2FB5`
- Fonts: "Space Grotesk" 700 for key words, 400 for the rest, 72px
- Layout: big white panel with a cut-out corner; keywords bold, rest light; hero image (hands, binoculars, etc.) overlapping the panel bottom; footer pill with URL/handle
